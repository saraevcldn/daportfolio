About Me

Hi, I'm Sara Erika, an Industrial Engineer transitioning into Data Engineering. My background is in production planning and inventory control, where I worked with operational data to support day-to-day decisions.

Through the FTW Foundation x Accenture Data Engineering Program, I gained hands-on experience building data pipelines using SQL, Python, PySpark, and Databricks. I enjoy working through data problems, understanding how systems connect, and finding ways to make data more reliable.

I'm a Databricks Certified Data Engineer Associate and DataCamp SQL Associate. I'm continuing to develop my skills through projects, practice, and collaboration.

My Background

I graduated with a Bachelor of Science in Industrial Engineering from the Technological Institute of the Philippines - Manila. My studies introduced me to process improvement, data analysis, and solving problems systematically.

I later worked in production planning and inventory-related roles, where accuracy, coordination, and understanding business processes were important parts of my work. These experiences strengthened my analytical approach and eventually led me to explore Data Engineering.

My Data Engineering Journey

My transition into Data Engineering became more focused when I joined the FTW Foundation x Accenture Data Engineering Scholarship Program. Through the program, I worked on collaborative projects involving data ingestion, transformation, validation, integration, and modeling.

One of these projects was ReachEd, an education data platform designed to help identify areas experiencing school, classroom, and teacher resource pressure. Our team received the Best Capstone Award for the project.

I also contributed to NYC Mobility, where I worked on weather API ingestion, data quality checks, and Databricks workflows. These projects helped me understand the importance of building pipelines that are not only functional but also reliable and maintainable.
